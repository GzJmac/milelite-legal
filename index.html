A simple routine

Drive. Review. Keep moving.

Let MileLite help with the repetitive logging, while you stay in control of what becomes part of your mileage record.

01 / CAPTURE

Let trips appear automatically

Opt in to background location and MileLite can detect likely drives while the app is closed. You can turn auto-tracking off in Settings at any time.

02 / REVIEW

Make the record yours

Review trips in your Inbox. Set the purpose, vehicle, business, tags, notes, and locations; edit details before keeping the record.

03 / REPORT

Export a clear mileage summary

Choose a period and filter your mileage report. Review the figures and confirm applicable rates and tax treatment with official guidance or a qualified professional.

Your location is sensitive. We treat it that way.

Automatic trip detection requires location permission. Background location is used only when you opt into auto-tracking; you can disable it in MileLite or revoke location access in iOS Settings.

Trip details are stored on your device and sync to MileLite’s cloud when you sign in. You can review, correct, export, or delete records. Read the full Privacy Policy.

MileLite provides mileage records and estimates, not tax, legal, or accounting advice. Verify current rates and eligibility with official sources before filing or reimbursement.
