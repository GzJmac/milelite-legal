MileLite

Automatic mileage tracking for iPhone.

MileLite is a mileage-first iPhone app built for freelancers, contractors, hosts, and small businesses that want clean trip logging, review, syncing, and reporting without extra overhead.

Current phase-one capabilities include automatic trip tracking, manual trip review, account sync across phones, Apple Maps trip search, odometer capture, and exportable mileage reporting.

  • Sign in with Apple, Google, or email code
  • Business, personal, medical, and charity trip categories
  • Vehicle management and odometer history
  • Shared ledger sync on the same account across phones
