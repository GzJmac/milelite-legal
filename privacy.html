MileLite Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 29, 2026

Home Terms of Service Support

MileLite is an iPhone mileage tracker. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it is stored, and why.

Data We Use

MileLite stores and processes:

  • mileage trips
  • trip notes and tags
  • trip start and end locations
  • route points and timestamps
  • vehicles and vehicle settings
  • odometer readings entered by the user
  • mileage rates and report settings
  • account identity details when you sign in

If you enable automatic tracking, MileLite uses location data to detect and record trips, including route points, trip start and end times, distance, and destination labels.

Account Sign-In Data

MileLite supports sign in with Apple, Google, and email one-time codes.

When you sign in, MileLite may receive and store your email address, display name, profile photo when supplied by the provider, and provider account identifier and session data. This data is used for account access, cloud sync, support, and restoring your mileage history across devices.

Google User Data

If you sign in with Google, MileLite uses Google user data only for account sign-in and account restoration. MileLite does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it except as needed to provide app functionality, comply with law, or protect users and the service.

Phase 1 of MileLite does not use Google Drive access for customer-facing receipt features.

Location Data

Automatic mileage tracking requires location access. If you opt into background auto-tracking, MileLite also uses background location so trips can be detected when the app is not open.

Location data is used to detect trips, calculate mileage, render trip maps, infer likely trip endpoints, and create mileage logs and reports. You can turn off auto-tracking in the app or revoke location permission in iOS Settings at any time.

Storage and Cloud Sync

MileLite stores data locally on your device and, when you sign in, syncs mileage data to a Supabase-hosted cloud database so your trips, vehicles, settings, and tags can be restored on another phone signed into the same account.

If you do not sign in, your data may remain only on the device. If the app is deleted before data is synced, local-only trip data may be lost unless you restore from an iPhone backup.

Payments

MileLite may offer paid subscriptions through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes billing and subscription management. MileLite does not receive your full payment card information from Apple.

Data Sharing

MileLite does not sell personal data and does not use personal data for third-party advertising.

MileLite may rely on service providers that process data to operate the app, including Supabase for authentication and synced mileage data, Apple services for Sign in with Apple, maps, and system notifications, and Google services if you choose Google sign-in.

Your Choices

You can sign out of your account in Settings, disable auto-tracking in the app, revoke location access in iOS Settings, edit or delete trips in the app, delete the app from your phone, and contact support to request account-related help.
