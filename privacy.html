MileLite Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 28, 2026

MileLite is a mileage tracking app for iPhone. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

Data We Use

MileLite stores mileage trips, vehicles, tags, notes, mileage rates, and report settings locally on your device. If you enable automatic tracking, the app uses location data to detect and record trips, including start location, end location, route points, distance, and timestamps.

If you sign in with Google, MileLite requests your Google profile information, including your email address, name, and profile photo, so the app can identify your account. MileLite may also request Google Drive file access using the drive.file scope if you later enable receipt or report upload features.

Google User Data

MileLite uses Google user data only to provide account sign-in and optional user-requested Drive file features. MileLite does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it except as needed to provide app functionality, comply with law, or protect the app and users.

When Google Drive upload features are enabled, MileLite uses the narrow drive.file scope so the app can create and manage files that you choose to create or upload with MileLite. The app does not request broad access to all files in your Google Drive.

Location Data

Automatic mileage tracking requires location access, including background location if you opt in. Location data is used to detect trips, calculate route distance, show trip maps, and create mileage logs. You can turn off auto-tracking in the app or revoke location permission in iOS Settings.

Storage and Sharing

Phase-one MileLite stores app data locally on your device. Google account tokens are stored using secure device storage. If cloud sync, receipt upload, or account billing features are added, this policy will be updated before those features are made generally available.

Payments

MileLite may offer paid subscriptions through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes payments and subscription management. MileLite does not receive your full payment card details from Apple.

Your Choices

You can delete trips in the app, sign out of Google in Settings, revoke Google access in your Google Account, and delete the app to remove locally stored app data from your device.
